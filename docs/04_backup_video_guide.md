# 4. Backup video: 90-second screen recording (backup_demo.mp4)

**Software (free):** OBS Studio (Windows/Mac/Linux), or Xbox Game Bar (Win + G) on Windows, or QuickTime (Mac). Record screen only; narration optional.

**Settings:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, MP4. Browser at 100–110% zoom, F11 full screen, close notifications (Windows: Focus Assist on).

**Prepare:** open the live URL, Overview, India 2025 loaded. Do one dry run.

| Seconds | Show | Say (one line) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Overview, KPI row | "CGETS India: four layers, one working simulator." |
| 10–35 | L2: India 2025, then 2030 Target | "24-hour dispatch: demand line, solar, wind and the fossil gap." |
| 35–50 | Untick, then tick Storage | "Storage off: curtailment 337 and fossil gap 2,302. On: 113 and 2,103." |
| 50–65 | L3: AMI to 80, V2G on | "Grid intelligence trims the peak and the fossil gap." |
| 65–75 | L4 donut, rooftop calculator | "EV mix and a 3 kW rooftop estimate." |
| 75–90 | Assumptions table, Evidence | "Every figure has a source and tag." |

**After:** play it once, check it is under 90–100 s, name it `backup_demo.mp4`, and copy it to a USB stick and your phone. Keep the file out of the GitHub repo if it is larger than 25 MB (upload to Drive and link it).
